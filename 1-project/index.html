About HR Agency

Human resources (HR) is a business field that focuses on the management, recruitment, and oversight of employees. It is responsible for hiring, training, cultivating, and managing the productivity of employees. HR is also responsible for providing guidance on employment law, compensation and benefits, performance reviews, and workplace safety. It also monitors and motivates employees to cultivate positive morale and employer-employee relationships.
